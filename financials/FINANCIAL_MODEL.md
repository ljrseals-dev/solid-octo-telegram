# Financial Model Summary (Illustrative Base Case)

**Currency: USD**  
**Raise: $100M at t=0**  
**Assumptions are conservative and intended for planning only.**

## Key Assumptions

- Freemium conversion: 4–7% of active creators become paid
- Average revenue per paid user (ARPU): $180–240 / year
- Enterprise deals: $50k–500k ACV
- Gross margin: 85%+ (software + local compute)
- Monthly burn at steady state Year 2: ~$3.5–4.0M

## Projected Revenue (Base Case)

| Year | Active Creators (EOY) | Paid Users | ARR (approx) | Notes |
|------|-----------------------|------------|--------------|-------|
| 1    | 40,000                | 1,800      | $0.4M        | Beta → launch |
| 2    | 180,000               | 11,000     | $2.3M        | Public growth |
| 3    | 450,000               | 32,000     | $7.5M        | + early enterprise |
| 4    | 900,000               | 70,000     | $18M         | Marketplace + plugins |
| 5    | 1.5M+                 | 130,000+   | $35M+        | Scale |

Enterprise and marketplace revenue accelerate the later years.

## Cash Position

- Starting cash: $100M
- Peak annual burn (Year 2): ~$45–50M (heavy hiring + GTM)
- Expected cash at end of Year 3: still > $20M under base case (before any additional raise)
- Path to cash-flow positive exists in Year 4–5 under base assumptions

## Sensitivity

- Upside: Strong enterprise adoption or viral creator growth → earlier profitability
- Downside: Slower conversion or higher competition → need for additional capital or tighter cost control
