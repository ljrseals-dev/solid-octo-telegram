# Cap Table & Raise Structure (Illustrative)

## Proposed Raise

- **Amount**: $100,000,000
- **Instrument**: Series A Preferred (or Series B depending on prior rounds)
- **Valuation**: To be negotiated (pre-money target range discussed in meetings)
- **Use of proceeds**: See USE_OF_FUNDS.md
- **Board**: Investor seats + founder seats + independent

## Illustrative Post-Money Ownership (example only)

| Party                    | Ownership |
|--------------------------|-----------|
| Founders & Early Team    | 35–45%    |
| Prior investors / seed   | 10–15%    |
| New $100M investors      | 25–35%    |
| Option pool (refreshed)  | 15–20%    |

Exact numbers depend on pre-money valuation and option pool negotiations.

## Investor Rights (Typical)

- Pro-rata rights
- Information rights
- Standard protective provisions
- Board representation proportional to ownership

## Exit Orientation

Long-term independent company preferred.  
Potential strategic interest from major music tech, creative software, or privacy-focused platforms.  
IPO path remains open if scale and margins support it.
