# Key Risks & Mitigations

## 1. Technical Risk
**Risk**: Achieving professional audio quality and determinism across platforms while staying lightweight.  
**Mitigation**: Pure DSP core first, extensive golden tests, early hiring of senior DSP engineers, hardware partnership testing.

## 2. Adoption Risk
**Risk**: Creators stay with familiar cloud tools.  
**Mitigation**: Aggressive creator seeding, open-source core, superior offline + privacy narrative, excellent UX, prompt export compatibility with existing workflows.

## 3. Competitive Response
**Risk**: Suno / Udio / major DAWs release local versions.  
**Mitigation**: First-mover on true offline + determinism + matrix control, strong IP and community, continuous quality improvement, enterprise focus.

## 4. Talent Risk
**Risk**: Difficulty hiring elite DSP and on-device ML talent.  
**Mitigation**: Competitive compensation, meaningful equity, mission-driven culture, remote flexibility, targeted university and industry recruiting.

## 5. Regulatory / Legal Risk
**Risk**: Evolving AI regulations or copyright challenges.  
**Mitigation**: Local-first architecture (no training on user data), strong privacy posture, legal budget, proactive compliance (EU AI Act readiness).

## 6. Execution / Burn Risk
**Risk**: Over-hiring or slower revenue → shortened runway.  
**Mitigation**: 36-month planned runway, staged hiring, clear milestones, regular board oversight, contingency reserves.

## 7. Market Timing
**Risk**: Generative music hype cools.  
**Mitigation**: Focus on durable professional use cases (reproducibility, privacy, offline) rather than pure consumer novelty.

## Priority Records (Investor Diligence)

1. Working deterministic DSP engine with golden tests
2. Matrix-aware classifier producing coherent prompts
3. Offline installer proof
4. Early creator feedback and waitlist metrics
5. Clear IP ownership and freedom-to-operate analysis
6. Detailed 36-month financial model and hiring plan
7. Security & privacy architecture review
