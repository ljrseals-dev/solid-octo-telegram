# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Phase 1 — Creator Love (Months 1–9 post-raise)

- Private beta with 500 carefully selected producers, sound designers, and composers
- Free lifetime Pro licenses for influential creators who publicly support the product
- Heavy presence on YouTube, Discord, Reddit (r/synthesizers, r/WeAreTheMusicMakers, r/AIMusic)
- Open-source release of the non-commercial DSP core to attract contributors and credibility

## Phase 2 — Product Launch & Community (Months 9–18)

- Public 1.0 release with polished installers
- Freemium model live
- Preset + prompt marketplace launch
- Partnership announcements with select DAW and hardware vendors

## Phase 3 — Enterprise & Scale (Months 18–36)

- Studio and enterprise sales motion
- On-prem / air-gapped deployments
- Mobile and plugin versions
- International expansion with localization and data-residency messaging

## Key Metrics

- Weekly Active Creators
- Presets created & shared
- Prompts exported
- Paid conversion rate
- Net Revenue Retention
- Time-to-first-render (onboarding friction)

## Partnership Targets

- Hardware: Native Instruments, Arturia, Teenage Engineering, Apple, Framework
- Software: Ableton, Image-Line, Bitwig, Reaper
- Education: Music schools and universities wanting private AI tools
- Enterprise: Game studios, advertising agencies, film/TV post-production houses
